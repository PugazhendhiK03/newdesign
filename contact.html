Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some common questions we receive from our clients.

What are your COVID-19 safety measures?

We strictly follow all local health guidelines. Our therapists wear masks at all times, all equipment is sanitized between clients, and we maintain proper social distancing in common areas. We also conduct temperature checks upon arrival.

Do I need to make an appointment in advance?

While walk-ins are welcome based on availability, we highly recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time slot, especially during peak seasons. You can book online or by calling us directly.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment may be subject to a 50% cancellation fee. No-shows will be charged the full service amount.

What should I wear/bring for my treatment?

Wear comfortable clothing. For massage treatments, you may undress to your level of comfort, and you will be properly draped at all times. We provide all necessary amenities including robes, slippers, and towels.

Do you offer gift certificates?

Yes, we offer digital gift certificates that can be purchased online or at our spa. They are valid for 12 months and can be used for any of our services or packages.
