About Us

Luxe Bali Male Spa

Founded in 2015, Luxe Bali Male Spa was born from a vision to create a sanctuary where men could experience premium wellness treatments in an environment designed specifically for their needs.

Our founder, Michael Tan, combined traditional Balinese healing techniques with modern spa therapies to craft a unique wellness experience that has become synonymous with luxury and relaxation in Bali.
